preferred.  For some materials (such as coals or wood species), the values tabulated are
typical values selected from a large number of results for samples of a similar nature.
The selection process is based upon the personal and professional opinion of an evaluator
after all property information for a material has been collected and examined.  Factors
taken into consideration are (1) description of the method used  t o carry out the mea-
surements, (2) characterization of the material, (3) number of measurements performed,
(4) treatment of the experimental data, (5) dispersion of the results, and (6) background
or experience of the laboratory personnel carrying out the measurements.
