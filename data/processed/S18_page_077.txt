Start-Up Time
Perry and Green [83] indicate that for a moderately complex plant, start-up should be about 25%
of the construction time, or 6 months in this case. Delta-T’s experience (described in the 2002
design report) with start-up indicated that a large grain-to-ethanol plant could be started up in
less than 6 months. Considering that this design is for the nth operating plant, we assumed a start-
up time of 3 months. The start-up period is not completely wasted, however. We expect that an
average of 50% production could be achieved during that period while incurring 75% of variable
expenses and 100% of fixed expenses.
