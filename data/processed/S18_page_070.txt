Once the total equipment cost has been determined in the year of interest, we must add several
other direct and indirect costs to determine the total capital investment (TCI). Site development
and warehouse costs are based on the inside-battery-limits (ISBL) equipment costs (Areas 200,
300, 400, and 500) and are considered part of the total direct cost (TDC). Project contingency,
field expenses, home-office engineering and construction activities, and other costs related to
construction are computed relative to the TDC and give the fixed capital investment (FCI) when
summed. The sum of FCI and the working capital for the project is the TCI. Table 27
summarizes these categories and additional factors. Harris Group reviewed the items and factors
and determined that they were consistent with other projects the company has completed. Harris

25%, so this was reduced to 20%. The project contingency was also changed from 3% to 10% of
