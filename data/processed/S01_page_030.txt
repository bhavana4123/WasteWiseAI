SUGARCANE BAGASSE; solid; Material is fibrous residue from sugar cane after the cane
    is harvested and crushed to extract the juice.  Mill-run bagasse contains an average of
   46%-52% moisture, 43%-52% fiber, and 2%-6% soluble solids (mostly sucrose).
   Substance is composed of pith (30% dry weight of cane), fiber bundles contained in
    pith (15% dry weight of cane), true fiber cell rind of stalk (50% dry weight of cane),
   and dense waxy nonfibrous epidermus (5% dry weight of cane),
