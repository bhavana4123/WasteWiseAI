Harris Group did obtain vendor quotes or other estimates for the truck unloading equipment,
storage domes, conveyors, and dust collectors. Although the final equipment list assembled by
Harris Group does not completely match the INL design report, the capital and operating costs
for Area 100 are assumed to be included in the MYPP feedstock cost and are not included in the
total capital investment (TCI) calculation; therefore they do not affect the MESP.

The truck scales were quoted by St. Louis Scale, and the truck dumpers and hoppers were quoted
by Jeffrey Rader. The storage domes, each with a 4,400 ton capacity, were quoted by Domtec.
The internal reclaim system was quoted by Cambelt. All other connecting conveyors were
quoted by Dearborn Midwest Conveyor Co. Sly, Inc. provided a quote for all components in the
dust collection system. The belt scales for biomass leaving the storage domes were quoted by
Tecweigh.
