   Smelting             The chemical reduction of a metal from its ore. Smelting separates

    Specific heat          The amount of heat required to raise a unit of mass of a substance by one
                         degree in temperature, under a specified temperature and pressure.

   Thermal efficiency    A measure of the efficiency of converting a fuel to energy and useful work;
                             useful work and energy output divided by higher heating value of input fuel
                           times 100 (for percent).
