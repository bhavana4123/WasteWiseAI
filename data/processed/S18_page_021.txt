determined conversions of specific reactions (e.g., cellulose to glucose) rather than using

energy balances.

The Aspen Plus simulation uses component physical properties internal to the software as well as
property data developed at NREL or from the literature [22, 23]. Since the 2002 model was
created, we have taken steps to reduce the number of custom-defined components and have
eliminated the external property databanks. A discussion of the components and properties used
is given in Appendix D.
